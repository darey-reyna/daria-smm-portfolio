# Glacier media

Recommended export set:

- `01-hero.webp` — strongest horizontal image, about 2400 × 1600 px
- `02-guest-instagram.webp` — feed, Reel cover or guest experience
- `03-hr-instagram.webp` — team or employer-brand content
- `04-linkedin.webp` — B2B or project-development communication
- `05-production.webp` — behind the scenes from a shoot
- `06-influencer.webp` — approved influencer integration
- `07-detail.webp` and `08-detail.webp` — vertical or close-up editorial frames

Use WebP or JPG for photos. Keep each image around 300–700 KB where possible.
Video can be MP4 (H.264), ideally under 15 MB, with a separate JPG/WebP poster.
