The task

Build a recognisable social presence and establish Glacier not simply as real estate or accommodation, but as a premium lifestyle brand — with separate communication logic for guests, future employees and business partners.

The challenge

Much of the complex was still under construction. The brand needed to feel complete and premium before every space was ready, while three audiences required different messages, content systems, tones of voice and KPIs.

Three communication systems

01

Guest Instagram

A content and visual system centred on the guest experience: Carpathian mornings, après-ski rituals, family scenarios, gastronomy, SPA and the atmosphere around the complex.

02

HR Instagram

A separate employer-brand channel built around people, culture, work and life in Bukovel, career growth and trust — rather than an endless vacancies feed.

03

LinkedIn & B2B

Business-led communication for employer reputation, company expertise, project development, partnerships and professional hospitality audiences.

Toyota & Lexus — Case Study

03 / AUTOMOTIVE & PERFORMANCE

Toyota
& Lexus

SMM and paid social built to move premium automotive communication from attention to qualified enquiries and test drives.

Organic social · Paid campaigns · Lead generation · Local dealer communication

45test-drive bookings in one week
11enquiries on launch day
$0.37cost per enquiry in selected campaigns
$2.6qualified RAV4 lead

The task

Build social communication that protected the brands’ premium positioning, made individual models relevant to local audiences and converted interest into measurable enquiries, consultations and test drives.

The challenge

Automotive decisions have a long consideration cycle. The work had to balance international brand standards with local offers — and optimise for lead quality, not for cheap form submissions that never reached the showroom.

Conversion logic

From product interest to a real dealership visit

  1. 01Model-led ad
  2. 02Enquiry
  3. 03Manager contact
  4. 04Test drive
  5. 05Potential sale

What I built

01

Ownership before specifications

The content placed each model inside recognisable scenarios — city driving, family travel, comfort, safety and design — so the audience could picture the car in their own life.

02

Creative linked to an offer

I developed ad concepts, copy, offers, visual briefs and model-specific messages, then evaluated them by enquiry cost and downstream quality rather than reach alone.

03

Lead quality as the KPI

Audiences, formats and messages were optimised against manager feedback: whether people answered, had purchase intent and progressed to a test drive.

Content direction

Local production translated global automotive codes into a visual language that felt specific to the dealership, the city and real ownership scenarios.

Toyota RAV4 / Lookalike

A qualified lead, not just a completed form

For the RAV4 campaign, I built a lookalike audience from prospective-customer data and paired it with a relevant model offer. The campaign delivered qualified leads at approximately $2.6 each.

My scope

SMM and content planning · organic and paid copy · advertising concepts and offers · creative briefs · lead-generation campaigns · audience segmentation and testing · lookalike audiences · lead cost and quality analysis · sales-team coordination · local adaptation of global brand communication.

abmk — Case Study

04 / ARCHITECTURE & EXPERTISE

abmk ↗

A dual-platform content system for one of Ukraine’s largest architecture and construction companies — translating complex projects, specialist knowledge and process into editorial stories people can understand and remember.

Instagram · LinkedIn · Strategy & full-cycle production

2platforms with distinct roles
UA
ENbilingual communication
8editorial content streams
360°strategy, copy and production

The task

Build a systematic social presence that showed more than polished renders: the people, decisions, expertise and working process behind architecture. The communication had to remain credible for professionals while staying clear and engaging for a broader audience.

The challenge

The portfolio ranged from university campuses and residential developments to urban concepts, heritage and engineering. Every story required research, interviews and the right narrative angle — without flattening technical detail or turning the feed into a formal project catalogue.

One brand, two platform roles

01 / INSTAGRAM

Architecture made tangible

Renders and professional photography were combined with educational carousels, site video, team interviews, event reportage and behind-the-scenes production. Each piece explained not only what was designed, but why the decision mattered.

02 / LINKEDIN

Expertise in business context

A separate editorial logic for partners, developers, clients, candidates and the international professional community: cases, expert perspectives, partnerships, company growth and employer-brand communication in Ukrainian and English.

Expertise → content

A repeatable editorial process for complex subjects

  1. 01
    Research the contextProject stage, problem, constraints and the decisions worth explaining.
  2. 02
    Extract the expertiseInterviews with architects, engineers and project leads.
  3. 03
    Find the narrative angleA precise human entry point instead of generic technical copy.
  4. 04
    Adapt by platformFormat, depth and argumentation shaped for Instagram or LinkedIn.

Production under my direction

I defined objectives and formats, prepared concepts, references, briefs and shot lists, coordinated heroes and locations, directed the production process and selected the final material. One shoot was planned to supply Reels, carousels, Stories, LinkedIn and PR.

My scope

Strategy · content architecture · platform-specific planning · contractor briefs · shoots · scripts · Ukrainian and English copy · expert interviews · coordination with PR, HR and commercial teams.
