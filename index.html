Glacier Premium Apartments — Case Study

01 / HOSPITALITY

Glacier Premium Apartments

An 18-month SMM engagement for a premium hospitality project building its brand alongside the phased development of the property.

18 months · 3 communication channels · Bukovel, Ukraine

1.5years of collaboration
3distinct channels
20+photo & video shoots
15+media integrations
300+content pieces

The task

Build a recognisable social presence and establish Glacier not simply as real estate or accommodation, but as a premium lifestyle brand — with separate communication logic for guests, future employees and business partners.

The challenge

Much of the complex was still under construction. The brand needed to feel complete and premium before every space was ready, while three audiences required different messages, content systems, tones of voice and KPIs.

Three communication systems

01

Guest Instagram

A content and visual system centred on the guest experience: Carpathian mornings, après-ski rituals, family scenarios, gastronomy, SPA and the atmosphere around the complex.

02

HR Instagram

A separate employer-brand channel built around people, culture, work and life in Bukovel, career growth and trust — rather than an endless vacancies feed.

03

LinkedIn & B2B

Business-led communication for employer reputation, company expertise, project development, partnerships and professional hospitality audiences.

Content production

I assembled and coordinated photographers, videographers, designers, models and local specialists. Every shoot included a concept, references, scripts, briefs and final quality control. The resulting asset library supported social, advertising and PR.

Influencer relations

I led talent selection, negotiations, integration concepts and delivery. Collaborations included Kateryna Osadcha, Taras Tsymbaliuk, Olena Svitlytska and Ukrainian lifestyle, travel and fashion creators — selected for audience fit, not follower count alone.

Rizatdinova Cup — Case Study

02 / SPORT & EVENT

Rizatdinova Cup

A fixed-term communications project for a children’s rhythmic gymnastics tournament, structured across pre-event, live and post-event phases.

Project period: 8 November - 31 December 2025

The scope covered social strategy and account design across the full project cycle, partner coordination and integrations, real-time tournament coverage and post-event communication.

01

Pre-event

Positioning, account launch and visual system, tournament narrative, partner announcements and the publishing plan.

02

Live

Real-time editorial coordination, athlete and backstage stories, results, partner visibility and event-day coverage.

03

Post-event

Results, highlights, acknowledgements, partner reporting and consolidation of the tournament’s digital footprint.
